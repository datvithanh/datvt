small talk is not fluff. its scanning, gauging for tone, interests, boundaries, stress leakage, etc before diving into depth

people, esp neurodivergent who dive into deep personal conversations off the bat are more susceptible to relational exploit

small talk as behavior scanning is a good cogsec practice and its learnable

"normal people" who dont think too much about meta analysis, culture, \[your own intellectual interest], etc also have depth and a rich interior. pay attention, you might be surprised
