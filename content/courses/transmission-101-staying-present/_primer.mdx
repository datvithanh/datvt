## What Is Transmission?

Transmission-based practice means direct contact with experience as it unfolds, without leaning on technique, maps, or a target state. It asks two things of you: a willingness to be seen, and a willingness to stay open when you don't yet know what's being pointed at.

## How to Move Through It

- **Set aside at least three hours per lesson.** Two hours, uninterrupted, for the teaching — plus roughly an hour afterward for reflection. Meditate along with the video, then take time to reflect on your own.
- **Max one lesson per day.** Let each teaching land before you move to the next.
- **Treat the Reading Material on the Lesson as homework.** It is a study of its own, don't feel like you need to comprehend everything to continue to the next transmission. The Transmissions will help you digest the materials.
- **Watch the discussion videos as another transmission.** Feel into the others as they ask questions and speak. Stay open to direct relating.
- **Don't rush.** This isn't material to get through. Some teachings will ask you to come back to them — returning is the practice working, not a sign you fell behind.
